Bayesian filter comparison

GRU: hₜ = GRU(xₜ, hₜ₋₁)
p(goal | history) = softmax(W hₜ + b)
Bayes: bₜ(g,i) ∝ P(aₜ | g,i,sₜ₋₁) Σᵢ′ P(i | i′) bₜ₋₁(g,i′)

xₜ contains position, recent movement, candidate-goal geometry, map and nearby-agent features. It excludes the simulator’s hidden goal and intention labels.

What drives the agents

controller

The neural policy chooses an intention and a move; the simulator enforces minimum intention dwell and then applies simultaneous collision rules.

Iₜ ~ softmax(uθ(map, position, peers, goal, previous intention, blocked count, dwell))
Aₜ ~ softmax(qθ(map, position, peers, goal, Iₜ))
L = CE(Iexpert) + CE(Aexpert | Iexpert)

Per-agent assigned destinations below are simulator truth, not recognizer inputs.

How to read it: the neural policy samples an intention and then a move. The simulator enforces intention dwell and collision rules.
